# References, dependencies and retained corrections

The source assignment is credited to Gunnar Wilken and Andreas Weiermann,
*Derivation Lengths Classification of Gödel's T Extending Howard's Assignment*,
LMCS 8(1:19), 2012, DOI 10.2168/LMCS-8(1:19)2012. The manuscript states the
types, literal trees, box, syntactic size, partials and abstraction clauses
needed for its constructions. The imported repaired domain, bounded-norm,
closed-evaluation and generated-source interfaces are stated explicitly and
proved in the named companion *Decidable Ground Comparison for Norm-Controlled
Ordinal Assignments*. None imports that companion's decision algorithm as a
premise of the source constructions.

The computability background is the usual effective machine enumeration,
bounded simulation and halting undecidability. The historical reference is
A. M. Turing, *On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the
Entscheidungsproblem*, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society (2)
42 (1936–37), 230–265, DOI 10.1112/plms/s2-42.1.230. The modern completeness
claim for the constructed control is proved by the local reduction and
countervaluation enumeration, not attributed as a literal statement of
Turing's paper.

The retained material comes from entry 09 and the source diagnostics in
entry 05 at public commit 4028a9e34506f4339af2992749464cf2d4d55eb9. The
functional-input polynomial-semiring reduction, including its explicit
unchanged-standard-control corollary, is retained in the main numerical paper
and not repeated here. The appendix retains the concrete
literal-policy witness, including its actual syntactic multipliers.

The reserve-transport proof now handles both associations of the active
three-term partial. A right-associated free cluster need not preserve the
older claimed extra reserve of two. The correct nondecrease inequality is
proved and suffices for the original-budget construction. The abstraction
multiplier and source objects are unchanged.

Unit observations are not assumed to be represented by an all-one closed
term. Carrier scalars are kept, positive fields are protected by the
generated-source invariant, and finite-prefix branches are included in the
whole-head graph. The grammar does not admit arbitrary active functional
arguments. The additive-one control is fixed before the program index is
supplied and satisfies the full standing growth and hierarchy bounds.

Earlier formalization records cover specifically stated interfaces. They
do not certify this new assembly, its arbitrary-policy exposition, or every
imported statement at once. This is an AI-assisted hand-proof manuscript,
with no new proof-assistant verification or historical priority claim.
