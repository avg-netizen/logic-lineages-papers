# Explicit constraint decisions over the rational constants

[Paper (PDF)](paper.pdf) · [Canonical TeX](paper.tex) · [Sources and prior art](SOURCES.md) · [Verification](VERIFICATION.md)

This eight-page paper gives two exact decision rules for the constrained-pair predicate of Miller–Ovchinnikov–Trushin over Q with zero derivation. For irreducible first-order p(Y,DY), the rule excludes exactly its constant solutions using the squarefree part of p(Y,0). For f(D)Y with rationally independent spectral roots, the rule is divisibility by an explicit product of linear forms, computed over Q by a resultant.

The paper includes both directions, designated-root isomorphisms over Q, algebraic constructions of all required solutions, a dependent-spectrum counterexample and a recognizable cyclotomic family of unbounded orders. It is a restricted decision paper; the full constraint-set problem remains outside its conclusions.

Classical differential algebra may already contain equivalent ingredients or criteria. A bounded literature check did not establish historical novelty, so the manuscript makes no priority claim. Its purpose is to present the exact statements and complete arguments for assessment.
