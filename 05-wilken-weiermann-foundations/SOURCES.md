# Mathematical sources and division of work

Wilken and Weiermann, *Derivation lengths classification of Gödel's T extending
Howard's assignment* (LMCS, 2012), supplies the original calculus and assignment,
the norm inequalities, admissible-expression domain and published effectiveness
question. The paper specifies the recursor argument order explicitly. The
classical norm-controlled predecessor framework is cited to Buchholz, Cichon
and Weiermann. Published ordinal analysis is cited to Pohlers and Simmons;
its new application to the particular syntactic ordering is proved in the
included ordering companion.

The present manuscript proves the maximum-predecessor recursion, collapse
recurrence, complete-count decomposition, finite-tail separation and actual
source translation. Its countervaluation-bound equivalence and elementary
observability and cyclic-binary pairing arguments are also proved here.
The companion summaries are navigation to complete proofs in the five included
manuscripts, not references to unavailable research notes.

The proof dependencies are: published assignment and norm theorems → this
foundation; this foundation → the five companions. Later sections of this
paper explain those companion results, but none is used to prove its own
foundational lemmas. In particular, the two residue proofs printed in the
multivariate and counting papers do not assume a comparison decider.
