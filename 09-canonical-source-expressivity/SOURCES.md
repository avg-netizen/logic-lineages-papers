# Mathematical sources and division of work

The canonical assignment and its norm domain are those of Wilken–Weiermann
(LMCS, 2012), with the ground recursion test first. The included foundations
paper states the literal rules, proves the generated-source invariant,
closed evaluator and fixed-tail separation, and supplies the common interface.

Narendran's polynomial-semiring linear-equation undecidability theorem, with
Dong's related treatment, is published background for the functional-input
reduction. The imported assertion concerns linear systems with integer
polynomial coefficients and unknowns in the nonnegative polynomial semiring.
No stronger completeness grade is assumed for it.

The arithmetic gadgets, Boolean compiler, observation identities, source
construction and original-head consumer are proved locally. The functional
undecidability proof includes the signed-pair compiler, paid scalar alignment,
norm-checked vector detector, zero-pattern reduction and actual closed kernel
lift. The control-sensitivity proof includes the primitive recursive control,
all required growth estimates, halting interpretation and nonperiodicity
argument. None is replaced by a private-note citation.

The dependency structure is one way: foundations → this paper. The reference
to the multivariate companion concerns its contrasting positive results; no
multivariate decision theorem is used to prove the reductions here. The
overview later uses this paper's compiler to express actual complete-count
attainment; that observation does not prove any lemma used by the compiler.
