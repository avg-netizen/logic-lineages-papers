## Reader-facing dependencies

- Cynthia Kop, *Higher Order Termination* (2012), Definition 5.19 and the preceding simultaneous-application operation; its strong-pair theorems and numbered auxiliary reduction provide published background.
- Wolfram Pohlers, *Proof Theory* (2009), Lemma 6.5.2, Definition 9.1.2 and Theorems 9.5.5–9.5.6 give the individual accessibility-norm bound.
- Harold Simmons, *A comparison of two systems of ordinal notations* (2004), and *Fruitful and helpful ordinal functions* (2008), supply the precisely matched finite-type extractor calibration.
- The included foundations and source-expressivity papers explain the separate canonical numerical comparison problem; they are not private conventions.

## Consolidated arguments

The paper reconstructs the finite observation domains, naturality and coherence, fixed-point operator, substitution and adequacy proofs. It then proves type and free-variable confinement, exact ranked RPO agreement, original reduction orientation, the finite-type arithmetic accessibility simulation, and a new native-recursion interpretation of the published extractor sequence. The cofinal family uses native recursors; a pure-term lower theorem for another relation is not imported.
