## Consolidated arguments

The paper gives the complete positive normal form using the equal-norm controlled-strictness endpoint proof, then the all-tail one-input finite-zero argument. It proves the exact prefix and ordered-control count lemmas, whole-profile reserve consumer, cross-mass and limit-mass packing, successor composition and count cocycle, strict mass induction, and the mutual sign/cut-certificate algorithm with its termination measure. These do not require an inaccessible appendix or an oracle from an earlier working document.
