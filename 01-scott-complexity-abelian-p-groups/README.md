# Scott complexity of countable abelian p-groups

[Full paper (PDF)](paper.pdf) · [Canonical TeX](paper.tex) · [Source guide](SOURCES.md) · [Checks](VERIFICATION.md)

The paper classifies the exact Scott complexity of every countable reduced abelian p-group from its length and bounded terminal subgroup. It also gives explicit successor difference-complexity examples and proves that no countable abelian p-group, with or without a divisible part, has exact pure Sigma complexity.

This answers Questions 5.1 and 5.2 of Alvir–Csima–MacLean in arXiv:2407.06940v1, and supplies explicit rank-gap examples for Question 1 of Gonzalez–Rossegger–Turetsky, arXiv:2606.15205v2. Their earlier abstract existence result is credited. Individual nonreduced complexity and the general algebraic pointed back-and-forth problem remain outside the classification.

Build with `python3 build_pdf.py` in this directory. Tectonic or pdfLaTeX is required; a new Tectonic installation can use `--online` to obtain TeX resources. No parent directory, private proof note, or custom vault macro is needed. The PDF uses Computer Modern with AMS mathematical symbols.
