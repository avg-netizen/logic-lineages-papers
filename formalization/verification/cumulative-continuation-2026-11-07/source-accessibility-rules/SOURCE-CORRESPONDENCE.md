endpoint. They refer to the existing `SourcePresentation.Data`, its literal
ordinary-arithmetic compiler graphs, and its single combined ID1 body.

`odd = false` selects the existing scalar carrier, positive-path certificate
checker, and even inductive tag. `odd = true` selects the existing list carrier,
direct-multiset certificate checker, and odd inductive tag. The definitions
introduce only names for those literal substituted formulas.

The normalized equation is

    membership(x) iff carrier(x) and forall y, edge(x,y) -> membership(y).

It is obtained from the previously proved original tagged fixed-point equations
by syntactic substitution and binder identities. The four further targets
derive carrier extraction, successor closure, closure from an explicit accepted
certificate, and accessibility introduction. Every endpoint may be a symbolic
arithmetic term, and arbitrary ambient hypotheses remain available.

internal derivation. These rules neither assume nor establish accessibility of
every typed source term, and they do not complete the numbered lemma.
