Printed source: `arithmetic.tex`, the original read, prefix,
comparison and domination components of a finite stage cutoff.

- On one actual original signed syntax state, retain exactly its
  ordered literal operand occurrences and signed constant. The
  raw common cost uses the exact original read cost, an effective
  majorant of the global tail from the sufficient alphabet budget,
  and the exact comparison ceiling at that budget.
- Independently the actual exact terminal sign threshold is bounded
  by the raw sufficient-budget sign cost. Recover the exact original
  syntax tape from the existing dummy cutoff state only; never
  interpret dummy zero values as actual reads. Majorate, not equate,
  the two original budget-based quantities.
- Combine the common and sign bounds by max, with primitive recursive
  composition. These costs require no valuation and assume no paid
  input. This does not yet compile the initial short-count cleanup,
  original row polarity or a family deadline, choose raw signed routes,
  or prove a full original high/closed decision result.
