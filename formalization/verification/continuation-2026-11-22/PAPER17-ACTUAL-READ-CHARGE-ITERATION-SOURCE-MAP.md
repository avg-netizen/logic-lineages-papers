The state is the literal integer constant and original read list at
one fixed f, ρ and rank set. At every node actual lifted reads supply

At an active node, the only extra supply assumed is the exact normalized
operands' original CountTailReady and common syntactic tail at their
own coordinates. These numeric premises are asked only at states
reachable along actual concrete edges. The theorem derives actual
aligned annotations, the actual maximum including maximality and
lower growth bound, and both original selected and transfer guards.
The split is the exact signed doubled coefficient of that same chosen
maximum, as in #900. Nonzero gives the exact original signed
decomposition and error estimate; sign/equality consequences use
only that terminal branch's own original semantic cutoff. No such
sign cutoff is needed for a zero rewrite or linear exit.

A zero edge explicitly retains the actual moved list, finite anchor,
disposition ledger, concrete equations and abstract intermediate
replacement, then the same #911 long-count cleanup and literal
new read state. It transports actual liftedness, original source
ranks and signed value, and strictly lowers the actual rank charge.
Charge induction derives one finite trace of these actual edges
and a terminal report, with edge count at most initial charge.
The final read list has the same original rank provenance and
signed value. No arbitrary edge or existence of a trace is supplied
as an assumption.

The reached-state numerical tail supply is not proved; in particular
the symbolic branching, enumeration of all future labels and tails,
and keeping all finite guards or small strips are separate. The
nonzero sign cutoff is not claimed to hold automatically. No
valuation-independent compiler/effectivity, global replay, complete
simultaneous branch handling, or dimension induction is asserted.
