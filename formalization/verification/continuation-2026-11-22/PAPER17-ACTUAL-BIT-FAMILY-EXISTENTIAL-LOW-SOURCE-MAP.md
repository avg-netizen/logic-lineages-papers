# Paper 17: actual generic finite bit-family locality, closed base and existential low reduction

Printed source: `arithmetic.tex`, `thm:arithmetic`, low infinite strips and
zero-coordinate base for the common actual signed residual family type.

- Literal union support is the only raw coordinate dependence: exact
  actual ordered row snapshots and signed bits transport when valuations
  agree on each used operand's true original rank. This is not an
  assumption of identically bound fields or whole-valuation equality.
- At zero union dimension, every literal source row has empty operands.
  Compute exact closed constants with their admitted actual signed bits.
  A rejected row or mismatched assigned bit rejects that conjunction;
  the finite family is existential over those exact closed clauses.
- A satisfying valuation with any actual used raw value below the
  supplied cutoff yields one genuine finite low child at that shared
  digit. Conversely a satisfying low child reconstructs one original
  witness at its shared digit: changing the sliced name cannot affect
  the child **because** its true union support excludes that name.
- All emitted children are of the same actual family type and strictly
  smaller than the true original distinct union support. This is the
  existential low/base layer, not an iteration or raw effective solver.
