# Actual numeric direct multiset replacement certificates

Paper06:564–567 and598–603 invokes DM(R); the convention is exactly the qualified DirectMultisetPresentation definition already accepted. R is actual positive quotient reachability, not ordinary local weak/strict comparison. Retained, removed and inserted lists are literal occurrence lists; source and target permutations preserve multiplicity. Removed is required nonempty, inserted may be empty, and each inserted occurrence receives the previously checked actual positivePath certificate, with removed-dominator reuse permitted.

The standard nested product/list Encodable instance codes source and target multisets by list representatives and codes the certificate. The numeric wrapper rejects every failed decode. It does not assert that every natural decodes, nor identify list codes under permutation; the relation is invariant under list permutation by the accepted supplier.

The three targets are direct checker soundness, existential direct certificate equivalence, and numeric existential equivalence with explicit successful source/target decoding. There is no bound on certificate search and no decision procedure for unbounded R or DM. Formal primitive recursiveness and ordinary arithmetic formulas, carrier/typedness restrictions needed for ID1, internal derivations, accessibility and well-foundedness remain separate.
