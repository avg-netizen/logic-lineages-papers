# Ordinary-addition relative norm: frozen supporting scope

Source: entries.tex, subsection Relative descent and the two comparison rules,
profile definition and eq:relative-norm. Source ordinal domain is below epsilon0.

`relativePrefix mu e` filters the actual ordinary Cantor normal form by e ≤ exponent
and reconstructs it using ordinary ordinal addition. `relativeProfile` is its
actual hereditary additive norm, exactly N(mu_{≥e}). `relativeCost mu y` uses
Ordinal.log omega y, the leading CNF exponent for nonzero y, and the separate
source zero convention N(mu). No natural sum/product replaces source addition.

`RelativePrefixNorm` checks reconstruction below epsilon0, literal retained CNF
weight sum, and norm bound for every cutoff e (including cutoffs above all terms).
`RelativeNorm` requires the complete identity N(mu+y)=N(y)+relativeCost(mu,y)
and both bounds, for every mu,y below epsilon0, including y=0 and complete or
partial absorption of mu. There is no unabsorbed-prefix assumption.

Reuse: actualOrdinalNorm_cnfEquation and notation correspondence are genuine
norm suppliers. Existing CompatiblePrefixNormProof applies only in its stated
unabsorbed case, not as a proof of this target. A structural ordinary ONote.add
argument or actual CNF argument must derive absorption/equal-exponent merging.

Supporting scope only. No relative-maximum recursion, propagation, gap/input,
equal-gap certificate, effective comparison or arithmetic claim is promoted.
No native launch before explicit queue grant. Independent pre-scope precedes proof.
