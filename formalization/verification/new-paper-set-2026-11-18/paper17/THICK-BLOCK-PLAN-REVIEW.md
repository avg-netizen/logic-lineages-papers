# Independent thick-block dependency review

The dependency direction is sound, but a full lem:thick target is NOT yet approved. The source's informal “undiverged/process a common block/remain fixed” domain must first become a concrete actual-trajectory predicate; a residue arithmetic lemma conditional on the desired visits is supporting only.

Require the same literal selected control, common original input and shared event clock for the two descents. Equality of relative limit parts does not by itself identify arbitrarily independently indexed budget orbits. Derive the common preceding norm from actual orbit identities at the common index. Define block boundaries by actual relative limit visits and retained ordinary CNF prefix, not a synthetic list of visit certificates. The target must derive the relevant successive top visits OR an earlier actual divergence, with the promised divergence before the block exit.

The global R/M/tail contract needs explicit inherited bounds: actual profile shifts lie within the R bound; M is the actual lcm through R+1 (or a separately proved equivalent modulus), 1+N(a0) divides M, and preceding budgets pay both target affordability N(target)+3 and M/B_f. Those inequalities must follow the source large-tail regime in the full wrapper. Merely assuming them at a chosen top visit is a useful local supplier, not full source coverage.

At rho and its predecessor top, prove actual absolute target norms equal c+N(rho), respectively c'+N(rho), from retained leading-profile constancy. Prove the intervening interval is the long case rather than a finite omega tail. Global no-divergence until this common visit must imply equal first-hit indices; do not assume their residue congruence. The small rounded-charge difference <M then forces equality, hence adjacent shifts and odd c+N(rho).

The budget u is the NEXT actual f(E) after the shared top visit. Derive its residue from the moving entry residue and the actual control decrement; never posit a fixed residue for all future budgets. Peeling to Pminus needs actual prefix-retention and additive norm identities. The source q>R+2 and N(a)<=R must be used to prove the required visits/retention and positive affordable local remainders, not merely carried as unused assumptions.

Limit exponent separation must become strict actual limit-part inequality. Successor separation uses v|X, X sufficiently large, a>=2 so a0>=1 and the coefficient difference occurs in an infinite block. Exact whole-state inequality alone is not enough.

Recommended split: freeze honest local arithmetic/moving-top-hit suppliers if useful; separately design the actual phase-domain and full visit-or-separate target. Do not promote lem:thick or equal-gap on the suppliers. Please send the proposed actual phase predicate before freezing a full source comparator.
