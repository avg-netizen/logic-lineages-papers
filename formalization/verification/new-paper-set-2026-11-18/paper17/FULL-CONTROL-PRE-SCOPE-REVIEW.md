recursiveness, G majorization, double-tower adjacent growth for every input,
strict next-G bound, eventual finite hierarchy bound and signed modular

and every actual nearControl b with B=b+1. The generic property is a conclusion

branches and all prefix parameters. Neither removes any source clause.

branches, exact aliases, recursive native evidence and final independent
review pass. It does not by itself assert the separate orbit corollary,
complete descent counts, entry residues or the comparison main theorem.
