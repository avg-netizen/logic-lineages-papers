# Independent conceptual review: ordered-maximum progress
Direction approved for staged exact supporting freezes, not a full tag3 solver. Read the proposed plan against the already reviewed ordered dispatcher and inactive transformer boundaries. The identified debts are real: unweighted maximal occurrence need not survive signed cancellation, free parameter values are not bounded by the bound-name maximum, and plain/allowed suppliers cannot be promoted to arbitrary mixed signed Phi/H input.

First cancellation checkpoint must be explicit about branch conditions. Constructive coverage alone cannot promise strict cancellation progress on branches where the selected class does not cancel. Either the endpoint is conditional on an internally decidable zero-sum branch and returns noncancelling branches as explicitly unfinished states, or the full transformer must pay their genuine progress separately. Do not label guard enumeration alone as all-branch progress.

The measure needs special care: partition/equality guards and retained domain guards can themselves contain nonlinear occurrences. Freeze exactly what counts as the current state and prove that dropping a class lowers the complete chosen well-founded measure; do not exclude new guards by fiat or reset counts when copying/repartitioning. Per-branch decrease is appropriate, not decrease of the sum of all branches. Preserve original guard truth even for zero-coefficient summands, under negation, and distinguish equality of arguments from equality of actual mixed head values.

Parameter boundaries must retain the same shared valuation; no bound-dependent term becomes an independent parameter. Same-name and signed low-end cases, modulus-one/initial residue cases, actual mixed inverse boundaries and whole-matrix substitution recovery remain explicit proof debts. At this stage prefer a small exact atom-equivalence/cancellation endpoint with transparent conditional progress over an unsupported complete progress transformer.

A later full one-step theorem must prove closure/support/computable exact construction and strict decrease for every unfinished successor, including far branches. No arbitrary normalization, alternating elimination or numbered decision credit follows from this conceptual approval. No native execution.
