# Complete refinement syntax scope and non-circular proof order

Freeze all assertions of composition.tex lem:sharp-syntax before proof:
reflexivity, transitivity, two typed free environments pointwise related by
actual refinement, fresh opening, renaming, marked-root retyping, successful
partial-mark reflection, and actual successful full-call covariance with
pointwise refining typed argument tuples. FullSyntax conjoins every assertion;
no theorem is supplied as a caller hypothesis. No extended comparison occurs
in these endpoints. Original-spine application remains unchanged.

The data carrier is the reviewed actual finite refinement tree. Because its
lambda clause uses common fresh opening, direct transitivity needs a genuine
preliminary, not an assumption: derive equivariance under injective typed-name
renamings by tree induction, preserving explicit tree size. A finite swap of
two fresh names then gives independence of the chosen fresh-opening name.
This is proved without refinement transitivity or substitution. It permits
both lambda witnesses to be opened at one fresh name during transitivity while
preserving the smaller right-child measure.

Transitivity then follows induction on the right refinement tree and its actual
extra witnesses. Existing-extra composition inverts the matching target-extra
witness of the left tree; whole-owner composition retypes the whole left
refinement and uses the strictly smaller right whole-owner premise. No relation
cut or auxiliary admissibility enters. All finite witness sizes count every
base, extra-list and whole-owner subtree; the finite tagged auxiliary comparison
is separate and not asserted well-founded.

General two-environment substitution is by refinement-tree induction with both
environments arbitrary. At a lambda, construct a fresh name outside the finite
free names of the source/target and the images actually occurring there, change
the chosen opening via the size-preserving fresh-swap result, and mask both
environments at that new name. Variable images use the actual pointwise
refinement input. No global fresh name for infinitely many unused images is
assumed. Concrete free/bound substitution composition supplies the opening
identity. Root retyping and successful marking reflection are direct structural
laws, not assumed as comparison closure.

Full-call covariance uses that proven two-environment law while consuming the
common original lambdas. A right-successful call excludes the blocked-variable
branch. At original heads, append remaining arguments once; existing extra
witnesses persist, while whole-owner witnesses use the already-proved structural
transitivity and omission refinement. No unconditional staged/full-call equality
is used. Two-stage PrefixEscape is downstream and retains its explicit owner-
refinement alternative in the original-bound-variable case.

Already checked reflexivity/retyping are supporting constituents only. Existing
frozen MarkReflection may be checked separately while this full scope is
reviewed. FullSyntax/lem:sharp-syntax must wait for all exact endpoints; later
ordinary-target elimination, auxiliary covariance and cut remain unproved.
