# Dependencies of the native tower witnesses

The checked literal layer supplies the native iterator and its strict beta chain, the indexed carrier on the actual closed native subtype, the literal zero and successor basis, and the two reductions for the printed addition and multiplication. The isolated ordinal layer supplies the full-function semantics, pointwise realization closure, the full-prefix orbit and the indexed carrier induction. The actual typed realization and iterator specialization remain pending because of the legacy and Mathlib import boundary.
