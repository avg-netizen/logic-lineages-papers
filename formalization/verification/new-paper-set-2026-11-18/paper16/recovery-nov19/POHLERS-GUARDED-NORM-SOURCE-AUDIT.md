[Publisher DOI](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-69319-2)

Definitions 6.3.1 and 6.3.5 on pp. 86–87 apply the operator to the union of all strictly earlier stages and take the least membership stage as the inductive norm. In particular, stage zero applies the operator to the empty set. Lemma 6.5.2 on p. 90 identifies the accessibility norm with descending rank for an arithmetically definable well-founded relation on its field. The book defines that field by participation in an incident edge. Paper 16 uses a separate actual admissible-address guard. That guard includes possible isolated vertices. A guarded vertex without a predecessor therefore has rank zero and enters the first stage at index zero. This calls for the original guarded rank/stage calculation. Definitions 9.1.1–9.1.2 on pp. 157–158 concern an arithmetical formula positive in its inductive predicate, its corresponding set symbol, and genuine object-theoretic ID₁ derivability of a numeral membership atom. Theorem 9.5.5 on p. 181 embeds such derivations in the controlled infinitary system. Theorem 9.5.6 bounds the supremum of all these norms plus one by the stated Bachmann–Howard ordinal. The greater stage bound for an individual numeric membership is strict because its norm plus one appears in that supremum. The proof also gives an individual strict bound directly.

For Paper 16 the remaining link is a genuine internal derivation for the actual tagged arithmetic operator. The external stage calculation, the scalar projection from the tagged stage, and identification of the numerical rank with the same-window positive quotient rank are separate earlier comparisons. The external proof of membership supplies no object-theoretic derivation.
