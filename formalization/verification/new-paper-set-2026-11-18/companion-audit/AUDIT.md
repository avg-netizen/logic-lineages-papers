## 19: source expressivity and control sensitivity

The surrounding setting now quantifies a primitive-recursive f>=G with
double-exponential step growth and eventual finite-hierarchy bound, and a
computable admissible literal parsing policy. Matching same-label statements
therefore does NOT by itself justify reusing a fixed-standard-control or
default-policy proof. Inspect each definition's inherited control/policy.

**Concrete policy gap:** the new `lem:reserve` proof (realization.tex88–109)
handles both three-operand associations. With one active child, grouping two
free operands first guarantees reserve nondecrease but not an extra two units.
The old `GroundPartialCarrierTransportTargets.GroundPartialAccount` and
`ActualSourceGroundPartial` explicitly use `OrdinalSyntax.defaultPolicy`;
`OriginalGroundCompilerCodeTargets.ActualOriginalCompiler` likewise fixes it.
Thus old09 `lem:reserve` and `thm:ground-construction` cannot be promoted for the
new arbitrary-policy scope without a policy-parametric transport/compiler
bridge. Existing source renaming may be policy-parametric but does not remove
that hard-coded policy from these endpoints. Preserve the original box second
coordinate, multiplier and scale2^b; do not regain an easier theorem by changing
them or assuming carrier certificates.

**Concrete stronger growth:** new `lem:perturbed-control` requires
Gamma_b(n+1)>=2^(2^Gamma_b(n)). Old
`SourcePerturbedControlTargets.FullPerturbedControl` proves only
4*control(n)<control(n+1), plus prefix/distance/PR/additive margins/eventualF6.
The same actual control `Phi n + error b H n` is reusable, but the new growth
clause is an unpaid strengthening. The generic Boolean H scope of the old
control lemma is not a replacement for halting completeness in hardness.

The arithmetic/collision/observer/unit/head-chain statements are strong reuse
candidates, with their exact declarations recorded in the JSON. `lem:one-unit`
renames the size notation but retains the original full-vector multiplier.
`prop:actual-attainment` is a supplied-*actual* unary source claim, not a license
to introduce a realization oracle. It has no direct same-label old candidate.
Functional-input hardness moved to17; do not recount it as new19.

## 20: controlled descent and complete Cartier profile

Old10 fixed-standard-control count/transfer/numerator/selected-residue results
are substantial suppliers. For example
`GrowingBudgetTransferTargets.FullActualCongruentBudgetTransfer` explicitly
quantifies retained numerical mass, actual scheduled budgets and first-finite
cut; it is more relevant than an adjacent-input surrogate. Keep the finite
coefficient and actual norm orbit f(f(u)), not f(u+1).

**New binary transient and complete profile:** old
`BinaryPoleRemovalTargets.FullActualBinaryHigherPoleRemoval` concerns repeated
`actualDifferentialCartier` alone. It proves properness and all finite higher
pole removal, but not arbitrary mixed L0/L1 words. New `lem:binary-transient`
needs L0=C(zR) properness and pole-order ceiling contraction as well as L1=C(R).
The complete profile theorem needs all distinct roots in a finite splitting
field, Frobenius-compatible tuples, unique proper partial fractions, both
updates b->sqrt(b) and b->sqrt(zeta*b), scalar sum(b/zeta) inF2, and equality
for every continuation. No polynomial part/pole/coefficient may be discarded.

Old `BinaryObserverTargets.ActualSourceBinarySelectedParity` and
`BinarySourceTargets.ActualSourceBinaryObservability` are numerator-based
suppliers. They do not by themselves give the profile bijection or conversion
through the transient. `BinaryCartierUpdateTargets.ActualBinaryRationalCartierUpdate`
and `CartierProperTargets.ActualDifferentialCartierProper` are useful algebraic
bridges. `cor:scalar-profile` additionally needs the initial simple residues
and proof higher poles never become simple under repeated C. Do not replace
all-binary-word behavior by only all-one words.

Finite periodicity must use finite-field coefficients, not arbitrary perfect
fields. The selected tuple's exact period is not scalar-bit nonperiodicity or
full reachability of every numerator/profile state. New `thm:main` and
`cor:fixed-polynomial-residue` require assembly; no direct historical label.
