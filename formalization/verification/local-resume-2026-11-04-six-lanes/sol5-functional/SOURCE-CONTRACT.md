## Printed contract

The standard control remains fixed. Output is ground, free names have levels
at most one, and internal levels are at most two for finitely many comparison
queries. The single-query branch has internal level at most three and the same
polynomial-system unsatisfiability input. Both strict and weak full universal
canonical comparison are asserted undecidable. No all-ground-input hardness,
many-one completeness of the input problem, arbitrary products of unknown
polynomials, or collapse primitive added to source syntax is being claimed.

The input is a finite simultaneous system sum_i a_ji(X) p_i(X) = g_j(X), with
fixed integer-polynomial coefficients and unknowns p_i in N[X], including zero.
One shared unknown index must survive every equation, zero pattern, lift and
projection. A system of SignedPair.Expression faithfully represents these fixed
linear operations; it cannot multiply two unknowns. A source coding/adapter
and computability proof remain required for the final decision-theoretic claim.
