# Balanced collected large-branch dominance

Source: paper08 `thm:balanced`, `eq:balanced-row` and `eq:balanced-L`, specifically the paragraph ordering actual head outputs, collecting equal head/complete-input keys, and deciding the sign by the last surviving coefficient. This component assembles accepted literal collection, original threshold budgets, actual head-key windows, surviving dominance, and same-certificate affine cancellation descent.

The input is the original signed row. Its only generic domain premises are well-formed use of the complete shared basis, genuine heads, and every original complete input being at least the original row's threshold. Distinct keys, nonzero coefficients, a selected minimum input, and a greatest-key member are not supplied. The empty original row is allowed. Zero original coefficients and duplicate original keys are allowed.

`GreatestSurvivor` records literal membership in the accepted collected list and the absence of any strictly greater actual ordinal-head/complete-input key in that list. It does not order the numeric `Collection.key` encoding, replace actual heads by parameters, or build sign into a definition. A nonempty collected list must have such a member, and its coefficient must be nonzero. The assembly may derive a finite minimum complete input and a finite maximum actual atom value, then use the accepted actual-key window to establish greatest-key existence. These are proof obligations, not assumptions.

`ActualCollectedDichotomy` retains the entire original outside remainder when all blocks cancel. Otherwise it produces a greatest surviving member whose signed coefficient determines the value of the original uncollected row. `EveryGreatestSurvivorSign` states this sign conclusion for every greatest surviving member. All large-input hypotheses still use the original threshold; no recomputed post-cancellation threshold appears in either statement.

`ActualSourceDichotomy` instantiates the assembly on the literal signed comparison row of two existing upper source expressions. Both sides, all atoms and the outside remainder use exactly `basisValues k Γ C ν` from the same certificate and raw valuation. Well-formedness follows from the source grammar rather than becoming a new premise. Genuine heads are exposed as the two source-expression genuineness premises. No independence of basis entries or raw variables is assumed. The cancellation branch is the exact integer difference of the two literal `Affine.lowerPart` terms under the original lower certificate `C.1`; the surviving branch includes existence and the sign for every greatest actual survivor. The semantic assertion does not require a new genuineness premise on the lower certificate.

This is only the semantic large branch and the exact cancellation alternative. It does not claim a finite guarded-preorder formula, a raw-code compiler for collection or comparison, small-input exception enumeration, support preservation after cancellation, the full balanced translation, or Snyatkov decidability. Original pair support remains ambient data for the later finite-exception construction.
