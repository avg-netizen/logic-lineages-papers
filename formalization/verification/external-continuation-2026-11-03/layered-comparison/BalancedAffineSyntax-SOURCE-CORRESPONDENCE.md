# Balanced affine source closure and lower-level descent

Source: paper08 `thm:balanced`, the head-free/all-cancelled preceding-level remainder and the concluding statement that all large guards are affine comparisons of the actual common basis.

Numerals, addition, and literal finite repeated addition preserve the existing Syntax Γ under the SAME certificate. No new grammar or independent variable is introduced. Natural scale zero becomes the empty-support zero expression; exact support membership records nonzero multiples and unions. Genuine-head validity follows from the actual source components.

The outside part is a concrete Syntax Γ expression built from the original constant, outside coefficients, and C.2 basis terms under C.1. The complete head argument is another concrete Syntax Γ expression, including actual ordinal-head norm, offset and every positive basis coefficient. Their values and supports are proved exactly for all shared raw valuations, including empty bases and repeated/dependent entries.

The all-cancelled signed row therefore descends to the difference of two literal preceding-level expressions, not merely an integer-valued remainder oracle. Original pair support must still be retained for finite exceptions. This component does not claim raw-code computability of these constructors, the complete balanced formula compiler, finite exception enumeration, or Snyatkov decidability.
