# One-exchange presentation of guarded primary equivalence

The actual WindowPrimary generators are retained unchanged at the root: equivalent heads with both literal head-window guards and actual typed base permutation. Ordinary result equality, numbered labels/results and ordered extras are unchanged. One exchange is closed under exactly one recursive context, including either side of auxiliary application. ArgumentExchange changes one term or one tail, never several positions at once. It has no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity constructor.

Chain is the ordinary reflexive-transitive closure, presented by reflexivity, one edge and concatenation. The four targets assert actual primary erasure of an exchange, genuine exchange symmetry, and exact equality of its closure with existing WindowPrimary and WindowArgumentPrimary. No confinement, endpoint bound, decision oracle or typing premise is added. Symmetry, congruence-path lifting and argument transitivity must be derived in the proof.

This gives the actual edge presentation needed for later finite candidate reachability. It does not decide edges, reachability, primary equivalence, or exact class filtering. It changes no accepted reduction/comparison semantics and makes no transitivity claim about the original comparison relation.
