# Paper 16: actual left-preferred dominator branch on the literal append tape

Given an actual original scratch append and genuine membership of the
same dominator key in its output, object disjunction elimination yields
membership in the left tape, or nonmembership in the left together with
membership in the right. This is the deterministic selection formula
used by both complementary open row filters. The test does not remove
a matching dominator or impose uniqueness.
