`frameGood`, which compares exact slots 0, 1 and 3. Its only recursive
child key is from slot 2. With a changed argument its actual fallback
is the original base at the two parents, not the original base at the
arguments. One still needs an actual open law producing the forward and
backward base-parent reports from the original same-function argument
row evidence, or a separately selected physically certified base
with that open law and exact correspondence. A meta all-numeral
calibration does not provide an arbitrary open derivation. The
cover/window/measure comparisons and the syntactic equality stutter
of DirectedSeen cannot create such a base-parent report. This package
does not claim unsoundness or impossibility of that open law.
