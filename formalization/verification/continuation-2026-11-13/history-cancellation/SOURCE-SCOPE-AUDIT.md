## Four-sector proof and discharged hypotheses

InjectivityProof obtains internal additive splits for both pairs and eliminates all four combinations. The lower sector means a<b; upper means b<=a, including the diagonal. SectorProof uses the original pair branch equations and IntervalProof to place each output between major^2 and major^2+2*major. SeparationProof.sameMajor derives equality of shell majors from output equality, internal trichotomy and strict separation of distinct shell intervals.

In same-sector branches, OffsetProof cancels common square/major summands using internally derived addition cancellation. In cross-sector branches, the hypothetical collision forces a lower offset to be at least its major; combined with the strict lower-sector bound, internal strict irreflexivity yields contradiction. These sector and interval premises are locally introduced and discharged. They are not hidden assumptions in PairInjective.
