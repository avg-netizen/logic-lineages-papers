4. Prove internal iterator fusion/scan append equations and task-stack framing, using genuine internal induction. The useful framing law is: a prefix that successfully parses one formula from singleton(1,n) consumes exactly that task under any trailing task stack, leaving the trailing stack unchanged. A sufficiently strong stack-append invariant is needed across intermediate prefixes; this is the substantive obligation, not supplied by the existing native StackMeaning proof. Retain the valid-A premise: without it an arbitrary chosen split A/B need not be the formula boundary. A companion quantifier law uses token10 and changes the task context to n+1 exactly. Do NOT reflect native exactTypedDomain into these internal laws.

5. Lift from the two-argument validator to actual lineValidator on arbitrary internal paired line fields (rule/payload unconstrained for validity).
