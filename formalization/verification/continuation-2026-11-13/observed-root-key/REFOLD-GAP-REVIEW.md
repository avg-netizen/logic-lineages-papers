# Remaining full-refold gap: read-only review

No new specification or theorem is claimed here.

## Distinguish the two questions

Ordinary idempotent refolding of an already computed output is supported by the original traversal output invariant (`OriginalLambdaTraversalOutputProof.invariantAtInput`, whose output invariant includes the actual canonical graph at out,out). Together with the same-tuple final-canonical equivalence and graph functionality, this is not the substantive cross-input canonical-coherence gap.

The meaningful remaining question is equality of full folded endpoints of distinct related inputs, in particular the erased original input and erased final output at the actual deleted parameter. It is stronger than the new common hereditary-evaluator-output relation and stronger than equality of the once-minimum outputs.

## Why once-minimum agreement is insufficient by itself

`OriginalLambdaTraversalSelection.canonical rawData window` is the actual structural fold of its selected leaf. `OriginalLambdaTraversalInternal` opens application and lambda constructors, recursively processes children, and rebuilds those constructors. It calls the minimum-derived selected leaf only at OTHER tags. Therefore no definition or inspected theorem says this complete traversal is merely a postcomposition of the whole-input once-minimum. Assuming such factorization would skip the central proof obligation.

The new root-key preservation uses the preminimum structural key. This key is not an injective encoding of the original input and is not the later final canonical Object. Equal marks cannot directly justify equality of final outputs by program functionality, because the two programs have different inputs.

## Candidate substantive route

1. Prove selected OTHER-leaf output coherence at the SAME `ObservedPut.refined` tuple under an explicit genuine all-position admitted relation. Actual minimum coherence supplies equal minimum candidates, but the rank/activation/sidecar guards must also be proved aligned; observer preservation alone is not output equality.
2. Derive constructor-case alignment and actual child relations from the root structural-key equality and the existing all-position tests. Original structural core application/lambda reconstruction can supply child core equality by pair/constructor inversion. This does NOT automatically supply the whole all-position relation for each child.
3. Prove inherited all-position relation for application children and for lambda bodies at the exact encoded extended parameter. This is the nontrivial extra supplier, especially where deletion crosses a lambda binder. Do not replace it with a child-coherence premise in a claimed final theorem.
4. Use paired structural/payload induction with actual child decrease and the proved OTHER leaf case. Rebuild equal outputs at application/lambda returns; recover the unchanged full final fold through the existing same-tuple final-canonical equivalence.
5. For erased-endpoint cancellation, first derive this actual relation at the deleted parameter. One-step original evaluator erasure congruence or its coded-chain extension preserves an evaluator relation, not automatically this structural-key/all-position relation.

This route is a research plan, not a proof of covariance, Object fixedness/cancellation, or full internal I/C. No original source or runtime is replaced.
