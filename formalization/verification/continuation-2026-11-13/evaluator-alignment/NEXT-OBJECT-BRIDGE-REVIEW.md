To feed evaluator pairs into the SAME observed leaf theorem, one needs actual
original source/window/typed-domain admission for that tuple, equality of its
pre-key, rank agreement, and every actual deletion-position key/rank test.
The caller may not supply these as a purported final E-to-C theorem. The new
alignment results pay raw constructor dispatch and shared app/lambda fields,
not these additional observations. E's R remains independent of the observed
pre-key and original package p. Source acceptance uses an independent M;
its connection to the tuple's actual source/window is also an obligation.

On active domains the kernel can bypass old P, so merely noticing that P
is absent from E is not itself a counterexample. The real unpaid work is the
specific program/observer and domain compatibility, including deletion paths.
Likewise parentAccepted-to-childAccepted is unavailable for arbitrary packages;
the prior rank-swapping diagnostic must not be erased by the new E alignment.

by this review. Existing ordinary same-output refold idempotence is not the
cross-input extensionality gap and should not be counted again.
