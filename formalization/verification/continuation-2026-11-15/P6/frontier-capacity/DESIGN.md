# Actual original frontier budget

InternalCapacity combines actual FrontierRank depth+node<=seed with exact
SubtractionAlignment index+depth=seed and internal additive order cancellation.
All inputs, including index and seed, remain object-language terms. No typed
validity, decoded-list witness, native rank calibration or new graph is assumed.

InternalAlignedPreviousCoverage derives subtract(suc depth,index,seed) from
subtract(depth,suc index,seed) with the explicit nonsaturation bound, then applies
the actual previous-stage coverage theorem. Its table is graph(suc index,seed),
not an independently rooted child table. The corresponding current parent stage
would be graph(suc(suc index),seed), but need not be assumed to prove presence.

These pay exact index/depth duties, not the full cross-root invariant. Remaining:
raw tag7 inversion/positive rank, internal successor decomposition of available
index, strong quantified cache-coherence induction, and typed Some-definedness.
No proof before independent frozen review and passing coordinator preflight.
