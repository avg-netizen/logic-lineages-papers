The prior whole `lem:context` promotion was premature because its literal
context theorem fixed the hole environment. This gap is now discharged by
nine fresh exact RenamingEquivariance aliases, independently accepted and
included in the67alias focused cumulative aggregate.

Actual Weak, Strict, WeakMatch, StrictMultiset and ReplacementMatch are proved
covariant under arbitrary typed renamings. The original simultaneous Apply
and substitution covariance obligations are proved, including literal Select
and fresh FAbs. No comparison-preservation hypothesis is added to these
conclusions. The operation compatibility premise is the explicit commuting
substitution square and is discharged where the recursive relation uses it.

`RenamedContextStrictClosure` and `RenamedConfinedContextClosure` quantify any
original scope Ξ, hole scope Δ, output scope Γ and typed renaming Ξ→Δ. The empty
scope has its unique map into every binder scope; canonical weakening handles
fresh binders above open terms. Combined with all literal ordinary context
constructors, this supplies the missing global/fresh-binder source clause.
Actual typeSupport is invariant under renaming. Root reviewed these exact
contracts and the source correspondence before confirming resolution.

The historical correction is retained; old kernel proofs were not changed.

NativeCarrier handled its unused binder by direct syntax substitution.
Neither proof depended on the missing general bridge. No arbitrary-substitution
stability, ordinal or well-foundedness claim follows from this component.
