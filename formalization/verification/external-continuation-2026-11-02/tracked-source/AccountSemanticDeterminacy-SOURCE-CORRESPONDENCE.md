# Literal accounting semantic determinacy and universal soundness

Paper09 source-realization.tex128–159 specifies literal carrier accounting and
its later transports. The constructive existence theorem alone does NOT imply
soundness of an arbitrary transported Account. This component pays that missing
logical step before ground-partial reserve transport.

The same literal expression has the same ordered trace of carrier head VALUES,
finite c, occurrence-list length and integer reserve in any two Account proofs.
Actual unary source labels may differ; no equality or uniqueness of source
syntax is claimed. Ordered lists retain every duplicate occurrence and their
order. The free branch is disjoint from active branches by actual tracked-name
activity; finite and nonfinite heads are disjoint by actual ordinal value.
These facts, not a chosen-output equality premise, must prove determinacy.

UniversalSoundness derives the actual partial identity for every Account using
constructive existence and the proved semantic determinacy. It does not assume
that a chosen list of source labels equals another list. The complete actual
source target is nonvacuous: it derives its full assignment, actual partial,
existence of at least one literal accounting, and soundness for every member
of that accounting family, for all remaining ground observations.

This is a semantic choice-independence/soundness supplier for the original
accounting, not a normalization of labels. Ground-partial preservation of the
same actual labels, nondecrease and source lower reserve bounds remain open.
No whole reserve or global register promotion is claimed.
