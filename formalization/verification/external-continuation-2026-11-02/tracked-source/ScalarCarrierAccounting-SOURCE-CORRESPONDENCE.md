# Literal actual-carrier scalar accounting

Paper09 source-realization.tex128–153 (`lem:reserve`) defines an actual-carrier
multiset and finite constant by literal scalar recursion. This component proves
the accounting identity and actual-source existence only, not lower reserve
bounds or ground transport.

Carrier labels contain a finite typed context, actual original unary source,
entire actual default assignment (scalar retained), and proof of no free ground
names. The head value is computed from that actual vector under common units;
it is not a supplied ordinal field. Labels retain their own contexts, allowing
future transport to retain a closed actual source label without equating terms
of different ambient types. No context-transport theorem is claimed here.

Account is the literal occurrence-sensitive relation: whole F-free/F0 stops
have empty list and constant1; active Add concatenates both lists and adds1;
finite collapse head retains the tail list and adds its actual natural head
value and literal surplus; nonfinite head appends exactly one genuine carrier
with exact unit head and adds its literal surplus. Lists preserve duplicate
occurrences and represent the printed multiset without normalization. The
integer reserve is explicitly c-3*length, but no lower bound is assumed.

AccountingOrigin ties every produced occurrence label to an actual selected
nonfinite scalar collapse. ExactAccounting derives both a literal accounting
and actual scalar partial from the independently proved source certificates.
ActualSourceAccounting derives those certificates and the complete actual
source assignment for every member of the original tracked grammar; it has no
supplier/assignment/grade premise. The displayed equality uses ordinary
ordinal addition of the finite constant to the natural-sum carrier fold;
required natural/ordinary finite-tail algebra must be proved. Common-unit
observation quantifies all remaining ground inputs. Default policy is the
original fixed source convention, not an assumed policy independence result.

Ground-unit and ground-partial multiset preservation, reserve nondecrease and
actual source lower bounds1/4/5 remain separate. No whole reserve promotion.
