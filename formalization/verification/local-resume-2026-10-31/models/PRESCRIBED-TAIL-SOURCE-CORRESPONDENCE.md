## Alternative constructive proof

The paper invokes the general Zippin realization theorem. The checked proof
pays this source lemma directly and does not claim that the full general
Zippin theorem is formalized.

First truncate G at its actual cut: B=G/G_delta. Actual quotient-filtration
reflection, reducedness and the original length prove that B has exactly
length delta. Its actual socles at EVERY earlier stage are infinite.

The actual socle basis of T embeds into the actual socle of a countable
Prüfer direct sum E. The proved divisible-group homomorphism extension
extends this socle map to T; actual primary socle injectivity proves that
extension i:T→E injective. Thus C=E/i(T) is an actual countable divisible
group, with actual quotient map q.

Enumerate ALL pairs (beta,c) with beta<delta and c in C[p]. At the nth pair,
choose a vector in B_beta[p] outside the span of previous vectors. That
span is an actual finite set over the finite field ZMod p, while the
source socle is infinite. Prove the entire resulting sequence linearly
independent. Its assignment to the target c defines a real linear map on
its span. Two applications of the already proved divisible hom extension
give ONE actual f:B→C with the required socle surjectivity at every beta.

Take the literal subgroup H={(b,e):f(b)=q(e)} of B times E. Its projection
onto B is surjective. A transfinite induction proves H_alpha equals the
full preimage of B_alpha for every alpha<=delta. The successor step
corrects the compatibility error of chosen p-roots by a source socle
vector; the limit step is equality of actual intersections. Hence the
actual H_delta is the projection kernel, actually isomorphic to T.

The actual projection and its deep kernel give isomorphic actual Ulm
quotients at every earlier ordinal; these agree with G by the original
quotient theorem. The separately checked exact-tail-length transport
proves the exact length. A zero stage proves reducedness, and the literal
subgroup of a countable primary product proves countability and primary
torsion. No injective hull, Fraïssé limit, admissibility-class field,
balanced-map field, Zippin oracle, missing supplier or added axiom is used.
