# Paper 17 actual nonlinear image and prefix error bounds

The full leading value of a count is the control at the actual entry prefix norm. Its error includes the actual prefix count. The source bounds that count, the smaller full nonlinear values, and the raw coordinates by the logarithm of the largest current full value. [Original prefix error](../../../17-permitted-control-canonical-comparison/sections/arithmetic.tex#L95-L118) [Leading-value bounds](../../../17-permitted-control-canonical-comparison/sections/arithmetic.tex#L194-L225)

Every properly defined ordinary nonlinear field is an actual control image at a value at least its original raw input. The proof uses the actual first-entry hit, the proper entry's positive index, and the exact retained norm orbit through that index. For a source prefix, the same orbit proves the stronger bound that raw input plus count is at most its norm. It applies to the identity prefix as well. [Frozen target](../../LogicLineages/PermittedControl/SelectedNonlinearImageBoundsTargets.lean) [Actual orbit proof](../../LogicLineages/PermittedControl/SelectedNonlinearImageBoundsOrbitProof.lean)

For either selected control, adjacent growth gives the stated base-two logarithmic separation at strictly distinct actual image values. The original power lower bound gives the raw-coordinate logarithmic bound, including at input zero. This supporting result does not choose a leading class, construct the full signed error bound or cutoff, or assert the entire arithmetic decision theorem. The five exact aliases passed on the first combined gate, `1795327635388079692-9`.
