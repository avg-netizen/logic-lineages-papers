Existence of a collapse from its defining clause, abstractly: on a well-founded linear index order with a well-ordered value order, if for every index and every assignment of earlier values there is a critical value dominating the earlier values of small norm (Solvable), then a function satisfying the characterization (P) of #1463 exists, defined by well-founded recursion as the least such value. The dependent variant of the hypothesis is shown equivalent. With #1484 the collapse is then unique. Solvability for the paper's actual norm and criticality is not claimed.
