Two further components of the internal accessibility proof, compiled inside the ID1 object calculus for arbitrary open terms: I(q) implies J of the singleton of q (by the accepted slice induction on the scalar tag, with the finite union carried by #1487), and J of a multiset follows from I of all its members. Explicit arithmetic premises, not proved: the two union premises of #1487; the singleton of an I-carrier element and the empty code are J-carrier codes; no replacement step leaves the empty code; a replacement out of a singleton is generated from the empty code and singletons of descendants by union; and every multiset code is generated from the empty code and the singletons of its members by union. The two generation premises are induction schemas because the accepted list induction is for a different pairing than Paper 16's multiset codes. The lane's design note concludes there is no general shortcut for the open-term arithmetic obligations, and proposes a symbolic-evaluation library that would mechanize roughly half of them.
