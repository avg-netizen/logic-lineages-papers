A component of the internal accessibility proof (lem:arithmetic-accessibility), compiled inside the ID1 object calculus for arbitrary open terms and contexts: J is closed under multiset union. From J of two multisets and the union graph, J of the union is derived by the printed nested induction (outer J-induction on the first, inner on the second), using the accepted slice-induction rule and accessibility introduction. Two arithmetic premises are explicit and not proved: the union of two J-carrier codes is a carrier code, and a direct replacement step out of a union splits into two components, each unchanged or decreased with at least one decreased. Logic helpers in the proof are reusable by the later inductions. The lane's plan (opusB/ACCESSIBILITY-PLAN.md) lists six further skeleton components and nine arithmetic obligations before the uniform accessibility sentence.
