Three semantic layers toward the calibration at n = 3, proved from the paper's equations: for a monotone, inflationary function continuous at limits, fix gives the least fixed point strictly above its argument; for monotone inflationary h with omega squared at most h(omega), B_0 h at h(omega) is the least fixed point of the orbit map (false without that bound: successor is a counterexample); and the level invariant: every level B_0^beta(N_B) is monotone and inflationary, the levels increase pointwise, and each successor level gives the least fixed point above its argument of the previous level's orbit from zero. Frozen but not proved: the description of the collapse on the indices between Omega and Omega^Omega, and the calibration at n = 3 itself. The lane's note states the general level-l invariant (typed helpfulness plus a correspondence between the collapse on each index interval and iterates of the level functionals) as it would be frozen.
