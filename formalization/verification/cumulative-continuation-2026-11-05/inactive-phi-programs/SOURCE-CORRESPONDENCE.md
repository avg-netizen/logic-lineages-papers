order. The numeric admissibility test, exhaustive Boolean-mask list, exact
cancellation guard and nonnegativity/divisibility atom payload mirror the
unchanged typed construction. Targets require literal code equality and genuine
primitive recursiveness for these explicit functions, not merely existence of a
computable replacement.

does not prove the full maximal-name numeric compiler, hyperPhi coverage,
