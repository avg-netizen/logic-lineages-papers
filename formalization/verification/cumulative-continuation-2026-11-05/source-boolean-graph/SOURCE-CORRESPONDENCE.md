# Nested source terms and arbitrary Boolean polarity

This bridge targets the existential and universal closures of quantifier-free
SnyatkovCompletion.SourceFormula formulas, with the literal source arithmetic
terms (numerals, names, addition and arbitrary nested Phi). A single joined
term graph allocates disjoint witness names to all atom operands. Its total
functional graph is conjoined outside the unchanged Boolean predicate. This
avoids moving a merely existential graph across a negation.

The frozen statements require exact source variable occurrence lists, actual
predicate interpretation under the graph, literal plain-Phi matrix membership,
finite existential witness semantics, satisfiability equivalence and rejecting
Boolean decisions for both existential and universal closure. They do not assert
parameterized QE, arbitrary alternating source prefixes or quantified hyperPhi.
The program does not call its semantic specifications or receive an oracle.
