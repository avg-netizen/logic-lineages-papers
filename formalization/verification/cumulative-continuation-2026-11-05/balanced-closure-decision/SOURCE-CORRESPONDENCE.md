# Balanced comparison homogeneous closures

This supporting paper08 consumer uses the unchanged accepted balanced typed
comparison compiler, original shared certificate and original pair support.
The existential formula is the original comparison conjoined with positivity
on precisely that support. The universal formula is the implication from the
same positivity condition to the comparison. Outside coordinates are free and
unrestricted. Empty support, duplicate support names, repeated/dependent basis
entries, the empty source shape and every integer desired sign are retained.

Six exact targets cover the actual positivity guard, literal guard encoding,
literal typed compiler correspondence, guarded sign semantics, existential and
universal closure answers, and computability of the concrete numeric programs.
No comparator, support oracle or computability witness is supplied by a caller.
Raw algorithms preserve existing defaults; semantic claims concern genuine
typed source certificates/expressions only. This is not arbitrary Boolean or
alternating quantified balanced syntax, QE or full first-order BASE decision.
No source-register promotion is claimed.
