## Why the closed procedure does not settle the next gate

`PlainBranchSplit` covers a valuation by selecting a maximum of the actual
finite original name list and eliminating it. This is legitimate when all
those names are existentially quantified. In a uniform projection, a name
may instead be a retained free parameter; eliminating that name changes the
predicate. Evaluating all free parameters to numerals yields a decision for
one valuation, not a finite formula describing all valuations and not closure
under alternation.
