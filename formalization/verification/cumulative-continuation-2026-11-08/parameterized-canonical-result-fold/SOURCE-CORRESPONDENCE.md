The proof suppliers are the checked parameterized result-stack wiring and certified leaf/application closure, unconditional result-fold totality, literal constructor and payload-opening graphs, the source application closure and descent invariant proved for the newly frozen uniform selection, and full-language internal strong induction. Only the original child payloads must decrease; no packed child, normalized output or parameter size bound is introduced.
