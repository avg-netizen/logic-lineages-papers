# Joint-context ordinary-arithmetic formula semantics

Baseline: `4e97e8593acd48a08178bc33b4e76d8c2ed38ca0`. Paper06
`paper.tex` lines 538–569 calls for arithmetic H/R and DM(R), typed carriers,
and two unnested positive operators combined with disjoint numeric tags.
This component connects the accepted concrete joint-Gamma checker Programs
to the ORIGINAL ordinary-arithmetic compiler outputs and sourceBody.

The carrier target names SourcePresentation.checkerFormula, the binary target
names OrdinaryArithmeticQuotient.certificateGraph, and their right sides are
the ORIGINAL ContextUniform encoded predicates. All natural context/object
codes are quantified. Malformed object codes and cross-Gamma pairs remain
rejected. Existential certificates are the existing positive-path/direct
certificates; no checker, numbering, guard, or carrier is changed.

The closed-operator target uses the original sourceBody with its two numeric
tags and scalar/multiset well-typedness guards. The all-terms target uses the
original allTermsFormula. These are truth equivalences for every predicate P,
not assertions of the conclusion, least-set membership or Derivation values.
The final existential target supplies actual SourcePresentation.Data and the
unchanged ContextUniform.Calibrated equations together with those two formula
semantics; it has no caller-supplied calibration or derivability premise.

Nonduplication: SourcePresentationSupportProof and SourcePresentationProof
only prove analogous semantics for a FIXED Gamma. ContextUniformFiberProof
already proves fixed-fiber conservativity and cross-context disjointness;
those claims are not re-proved here. JointGammaProgramsProof supplies concrete
Programs but no truth theorem for the compiled formulas or tagged body.
The implementation may reuse generic checkerTruth, certificateGraphTruth,
closedPresentationTruth and taggedBranchIsolation, which are already paid.

Comparator targets and original semantic hashes are frozen before proof
creation and must compile first. Exact aliases, source-matched native closure
and standard-axiom audits are required under unchanged serialized caps.
No existing target/source, manuscript, register, dependency, checker, root
import or CI change. No II5, accessibility, well-foundedness, internal ID1
proof-tree construction or whole lem:id1 completion is claimed.
