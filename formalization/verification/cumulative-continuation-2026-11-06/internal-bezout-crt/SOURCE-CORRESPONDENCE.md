# Internal Bézout-to-CRT prerequisite

Supporting prerequisite for the ordinary-arithmetic internal finite-sequence coding route in paper 06, not completion of any numbered source theorem.
For arbitrary ordinary terms a,n,u,v,r,s and any internal context, a derivation of succ(u*a)=v*succ(n) yields a derivation of both genuine existing modular formulas for x=r*succ(u*a)+s*(n*(u*a)): x congruent r modulo a and x congruent s modulo succ(n).
The certificate is an explicit premise, not a disguised coprimality-to-Bézout theorem. Producing certificates from coprimality, arbitrary finite CRT assembly, beta sequence coding and the downstream internal comparison consumer remain open. No bound on x or remainder normalization is claimed. All arithmetic is derivation syntax, with no semantic Nat reflection.
