# Internal zero-or-successor decomposition

This frozen auxiliary target states, for every original arithmetic term r and every calculus context, that r=0 or there exists a predecessor whose successor is r. It supplies the case split needed to prove zero remainder for a least positive signed linear combination. It is an internal PA derivation, not semantic natural-number reflection, and does not by itself prove Bezout existence or promote a source theorem. Compile the frozen target before proof authoring.
