The manuscript uses the published DCB/FS/KT axioms, Equation (8)'s fixed point, and the original DCB-indexed provability predicate. The source's domains allow full-language sentences and one-free-variable selectors.

The local proof derives the needed provability implication and applies that theorem; no modal Löb axiom is assumed. Gorbow's Theorem 15 and Corollary 16 give published consistency background. The manuscript additionally includes the direct single-agent eventual-validity construction and checks all theorem rules and the actual UBF schema.

The local results are the DCB collapse, selector-sensitive KT equivalences, explicit theorem/common-knowledge counterinstance, and its finite-iteration consequence. Every local argument appears in the paper. These statements do not classify arithmetic-only necessitation, arbitrary stronger bases or every other extension mentioned in the source discussion.
