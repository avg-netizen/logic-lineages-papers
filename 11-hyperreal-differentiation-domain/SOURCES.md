# Sources and division of work

The source question is immediately after Corollary 10 of Samuel Allen Alexander and Bryan Dawson, *Hyperreal differentiation with an idempotent ultrafilter*, Journal of Logic and Analysis 16 (2024), paper 6, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4115/jla.2024.16.6), [versioned primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.14689v1). Definitions 5–6 fix additive idempotence and right-hand concentration near zero. Convention 2 and Corollary 10 fix the domain as classes with some representative differentiable on a large set.

The source's Theorem 9 supplies representative independence of the derivative. The manuscript includes the argument and elementary field closure for context, with attribution. Its main contribution is the universal properness argument: the persistent-discontinuity lemma, rational coordinate pushforward, exhaustive Hamel-coordinate cases, support-length argument, and the positive-infinitesimal and nonconvexity consequences.

Ordinary ultrafilter facts, existence of a Hamel basis in ZFC and elementary differentiation are background. The proof never uses infinite intersection closure of the ultrafilter. Merely accumulating differentiability points at zero does not replace the large-set condition.

The theorem answers the proper/full question uniformly in the ultrafilter. It does not compare the actual domains of distinct ultrafilters or produce a common or Borel witness. The full local proof is printed; source articles are normal bibliographic references, not bundled copyrighted texts or private dependencies.
